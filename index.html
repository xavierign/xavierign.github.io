FaCells

Teaching Machines the Language of Lines

FaCells is an ongoing exploration of how neural networks can internalize and redraw the essential traits of portraiture. The installation invites visitors to watch a plotter interpret facial attributes as flowing constellations of hand-drawn lines.

How the drawings come alive

Learning the language

Line drawings distilled from portrait datasets are translated into sequences of (x, y) coordinates. A recurrent neural network studies these gestures, encoding subtle rhythms in the way we sketch eyes, lips, and contours.

Hacking the neurons

Instead of stopping at classification, the model is coaxed into generating new combinations of attributes. Each neuron becomes a creative collaborator, remixing traits into portraits that feel simultaneously familiar and alien.

From code to paper

A pen plotter connected to the system acts as the medium between digital memory and physical ink. Visitors watch FaCells render in real time, revealing the cadence of the machine as it drafts each curve.

An invitation to imagine

Close your eyes and picture the concept of eyeglasses. FaCells makes that mental image visible, showing how machines can store, recall, and reinterpret visual ideas through accessible mathematics and a tangible drawing process. The work resists commodification—each piece is gifted to guests who share their experience from the booth. Come visit and claim one.
