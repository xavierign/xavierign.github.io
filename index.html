FaCells

Teaching Machines the Language of Lines

Spectrum Miami, booth #1112. “FaCells” are portraits broken down into 15 facial attributes—arched eyebrows, bangs, eyeglasses, and more. The plotter acts as a performer, translating a neural network’s learned attributes into physical line drawings.

The network isn’t a typical image or text model. It’s trained purely on lines, with each attribute forming a “cell.” It’s an imperfect learner—reflecting our biases, like what we consider “attractive”—and it perceives only contours, not color. The goal isn’t perfection but to question how machines interpret human categories.

Each drawing—a FaCell—is unique, merging algorithmic and human perception. Come explore how machines learn to see at Spectrum Miami.
